# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Project status

«Утка в номер» is the name players see in Russian (title screen, browser tab, «Об игре»), «Breaking Town» in English; Breaking Town (formerly News Fights) stays the working title in code, keys and the repo. It is a turn-based satirical strategy game: the player is a media outlet that manipulates true / half-true / false news to destroy a simulated city. Current stage: CONCEPT.md §7.4 step 3 — full simulation, headless bots, isometric PixiJS city with generated placeholder pixel art, UI per §7.3.

**Next version (CONCEPT.md §10, planned, not built yet):** the game loses money altogether (readers, reputation and cards stay), the first days open the controls one by one, and the town answers yesterday's story. Sections 1–9 of the concept and everything below describe the game with money as it is now; whoever builds a step of §10 rewrites those sections and this file in the same change. The last version with money is commit `5040fee` (`git checkout 5040fee`; a tag or branch `money-version`, if the remote has one, points at it) — it must stay easy to bring back, and the beta stays on the money version until the user says to move it.

## Architecture

- `src/sim/` — the whole game: pure TypeScript, deterministic, JSON-serializable `GameState`. Entry points in `index.ts`: `createGame(content, seed)`, `playTurn(state, content, decision)` → `TurnReport`, `listPurchases`/`purchase`, `previewPublication` (what UI and bots show before publishing). `tsconfig.sim.json` enforces no DOM/Node APIs here.
  - Turn order (`game.ts`): scandal recovery → late exposures of old lies (odds fade by `lateExposureDecay` each issue, forgotten after `lateExposureTurns`) → publish each card (`news.ts`: reach → belief → effects → word of mouth) → fact-check → ad income/upkeep (`economy.ts`) → next turn: city day (`city.ts`), real events become true cards, deal hand → outcome check.
  - All randomness goes through `rng.ts` with the state stored in `GameState.rng`; never use `Math.random` in sim.
  - Upgrades never mutate state directly; their effects are recomputed by `getModifiers()` from `state.upgrades`.
  - Resident ids equal their index in `state.residents`, building ids their index in `state.buildings`. `createGame` fills jobs by preference, then gives a workplace left empty one worker who may work there (jobless or from a place with more than one), so a small town never starts with its police or fact-checkers closed; towns without a shortage are untouched.
- `src/content/*.json` — all cards, channels, upgrades, groups, buildings and balance numbers. `validateContent()` (run in tests) checks consistency, e.g. card `titles`/`effects` are indexed by truth level and must be `null` below `baseTruth`.
  - News come from hired reporters (`reporters.ts` `findNews`, called in `startTurn`): one story per reporter a day: today's events (`generateEvents`, incidents, arc stages) take free reporters first — a specialist (who writes it `expertTierBonus` stronger, `CardInstance.expert`, «Эксклюзив» kicker), then one sent after its topic, then any free one; reporters investigating or watching the rival while it has lies to debunk take none; events left over go to `state.missedEvents` (greyed «— никто не написал» in the day's events); the reporters left each roll luck, then truth vs rumor (`trueNewsShare`), scope, topic and template (local true news = `fact` templates that change nothing) and a tier biased by nose within the template's `tiers` range (so the headline fits its level); a story whose template `requires` a building type is bound to one open building of it (`CardInstance.building`, names from `BuildingDef.names`, `buildingName`, shown as a kicker); at least `minFinds` story per turn. The player can send a reporter after topics (`state.reporterTopics`, `setReporterTopics`; `assignFocus`, `specialtyLuck` via `searchLuck`). Templates found or happened within `repeatCooldown` turns are skipped (`state.lastSeen`, `isRecent`), and finds, city events (`generateEvents`) and the rival's picks are weighted by how long ago they last appeared (`repeatFadeDays`; the rival also keeps `RivalState.seen`); templates may carry `titleVariants` (per truth level, one picked by `card.uid % n` in `storyTitle`; all four debunk templates have them); `validateContent` requires enough stories of each kind. `state.lastFinds` drives the UI search animation. **Reach** is the outlet's regular readers (`resident.follows`, `readers.ts`): followers of an owned channel read each story with `placementRead[placement]`; sales income = followers per channel at the start of the turn × (`prices[state.price]` + `adRate` + kiosks) (`incomePerReader`, `setPrice`; the price is chosen in the masthead's second row by a chip and menu without numbers, `renderPrice`, `priceMenuOpen`, `priceLabels`, coin icons `sprites/ui/price_0..3`). Who buys depends on the price and the resident's wealth class (`wealthOf`: working / pensioner / jobless, `priceBuy`, `buyWillingness`): it scales joining, and a reader who cannot or grudges to pay churns with reason `price`. After the issue `updateReaders` applies churn (based on `lastReactions`: doubted / exposed / untouched readers by issue level / empty issue) and acquisition (channel reach, upgrades, trust, issue level). The issue level (`issueScore`/`issueLevel` over `IssueStory` {tier, placement}: tier points × `issuePlacementPoints` against `issueThresholds`; sensational needs a sensation on the front page) is in `TurnReport.issue` and shown live in the UI. **Impact** (how hard viewers react: effect chance, outlook shift) depends on the story: template interest, effective tier (`tier + distortion`), placement (`placementImpact`), scope and the card's audience (`impactOf`, `audienceMul`; there is no topic fatigue any more — `state.topicStreak` counts days in a row a topic was printed and feeds the fact-checkers' watch `topicWatchOf` = 1 + `topicWatch` × min(days, `topicWatchMax`), in `previewPublication` and `verifyNews`, UI `watchChip` «👁 Под надзором +N%», hunt stories exempt); engagement and channel virality drive word of mouth. A riskier telling always hits harder: `impactOf` (wrapping `baseImpact`) raises a louder step's impact so that expected believers × effect strength beats the step below by `louderGain`, and in a hunt the stain too (not for the rival's stories); tested in `tests/sim.test.ts` «a riskier telling always hits harder». The audience is part of the card template (`audience`), not a player choice. There is no channel choice and no holding cards: a publication goes out on every owned channel (a resident sees it if any channel reaches them, `report.byChannel` feeds ad income), and unpublished cards are discarded. Trust (`resident.trust`, called «Репутация» in the UI and concept) and `state.scandal` are single values for the whole outlet. True news builds trust by placement (`placementTrustGain`) for viewers and `trustGainBystanderShare` of it for everyone else (`verifyNews`); `advanceCleanStreak` (in `playTurn`) counts issues without a half-truth or lie in `state.cleanStreak` and pays `cleanStreakBonus` to the whole town after `cleanStreakDays` (`TurnReport.cleanStreak` {days, broken, bonus}; UI `streakBadge` under the stamp via `renderStreak`, summary lines, the reputation window). There is no exposure meter: a caught lie (`exposeNews`) costs reputation (scaled by `placementExposurePenalty`, `exposurePenaltyMul`) and ad income, the game is lost when average reputation drops below `trustCollapse`, and high reputation lowers fact-check risk (`trustShield` in `exposureRiskOf`). `TurnReport.exposures` lists this turn's and late exposures (`ExposedStory`, with the turn of the issue) for the UI popup. Any number of cards may be published; each gets a placement (`Placement`: 0 brief, 1 article, 2 front page) that scales read chance, impact, exposure risk/gain and issue points (`placement*`/`issuePlacementPoints` balance arrays); only `frontPageSlots` cards may take the front page and `articleSlots` be articles (fixed balance values, no upgrade changes them) (`placementSlots`, checked in `validateDecision`); `rankedPlacements` is the shared default layout (best story front page, next article, rest briefs) used by the UI and bots. Investigations (`state.investigations`, `startInvestigation`/`cancelInvestigation`): a reporter skips ordinary search for `investigationTurns` turns, costs `investigationCost` per turn in `totalUpkeep`, then `advanceInvestigations` (in `startTurn`, before `findNews`) yields a tier 2–3 card of `source: 'investigation'` with a `target` group; its `stigma` effect angers believers from other groups and makes the target group lose outlook and leave, readers or not (end of `publish`, `report.stigma`). `Find.kind` tells the UI search animation apart from digging and exclusives. UI (CONCEPT.md §3.3а): the `dig` modal «Компромат на …» (`digTarget`, opened from the group cards and the focused node of the relations circle in «Жители») shows `digPromise(target, reporterId|null)` (headline from the template's `titles[0]`, days, effect in words from tension pairs and readers of other groups, cost, odds in words) and the reporters with «Копать»; the «Редакция» card's `digMenu` lists every group (and the rival while nobody digs into it) with its promise behind a «?» (`openInfo` `dig:<reporter>:<group>`); every explanation button in the game is «?»; progress is `копает N/M` under the portrait, a `.digging` line in the day's events («Завтра: эксклюзив…» the day before) and the `digNotice` plaque «Расследование готово» (`renderDig`, `digSeen`). Reporters (up to `maxReporters` = 5 of 14) are hired through the HR agency: `callAgency` is free for the first search a turn and costs `agencyFee` after (`agencyCost`, `state.agencyCalls` reset in `startTurn`) and puts 1–`agencyMaxOffer` random unhired reporters (by `ReporterDef.weight`, 0 = never offered) into `state.agencyOffer` (cleared in `startTurn`), `hireFromAgency` hires one of them for its `hireCost`; salaries are in `totalUpkeep`. At most `maxSensations` (1) sensation: extra tier-3 finds are lowered to tier 2 in `startTurn`, `validateDecision` rejects an issue with two effective sensations, and `limitSensations` tones extras down (UI `fitSensations`, bots).
  - The outlet is a newspaper only (`channels.json` holds just `newspaper`; the channel code stays generic). Upgrades (CONCEPT.md §3.5, `shop.ts`): forks (`forks.json`, `ForkDef` {day, options}; `UpgradeDef.fork`, `openForks`, `forkChoice` — one option for good), the board's draft (CONCEPT.md §3.5б, §3.5в: from `shopFirstTurn` every `shopRefreshDays` (4) days the `shop` window opens by itself after the search (`shopAutoTurn`), also on a fork's day («Решение редакции», `openForks` with `day === turn`); one card of an offer is taken for free and the offer closes, forks are free too (the head office pays) — board cards are never bought (`boardPrice`, `state.shopPicks`; a task voucher keeps the offer open for one more pick; `refreshShop` deals three more any time, before or after a pick, for `shopRequestCost` = `shopRefreshCost` + `shopRequestStep` × `state.shopExtras` (reset by the board's own offer in `advanceShop`, which alone moves `shopRefreshTurn`); UI `requestButton` «Другие три» / «Запросить ещё три карты · N ₽»), forks keep their price; `state.shopOffer` of `shopOfferSize` = 3 cards dealt by `dealShop` from `deckCards` — in `GameState.collection` (card id → level; null = all at level 1), not owned, not forks, of the mode, requirements met, a series by its next step, weighted by `rarityWeight[rarity]`; `advanceShop` every `shopRefreshDays`, `refreshShop` for `shopRefreshCost`; `onSale` gates `listPurchases`; the offer empties after the pick). The bank (CONCEPT.md §5.4а, `economy.ts`): `loanBlock` (one loan, only below `loanBelow`), `takeLoan` (`loanAmounts`, `state.loan` {amount, perDay, daysLeft}, `loanPerDay` = amount × (1 + `loanRate`) / `loanDays`, an upkeep item of kind `loan`, counted down in `playTurn` after income), `loanPayoff`/`repayLoan` (the principal left, no interest); UI `bankSection` in the money tab (`#bank`) and the red `bank-open` button in the `abilityBar` while in debt; a free board card is `affordable` in debt. Money goes to «Чёрная касса» (`cash.ts`, `cash.json`, CONCEPT.md §3.5в): only `base` moves (ads) are open from the start, the rest are opened by owned cards with `UpgradeDef.cash` (`cashOpen`, `cashMoves`; section «Чёрная касса», deck tag «касса»; `validateContent` requires a card for every non-base move), and the card's collection level divides the price by `cardLevelPower` (`cashCost`); `useCash`/`cashBlock`, cooldown in `state.cashReady`, the day a move acts in `state.cashEffects` (`cashActive`): `leak` puts a rumor sensation (maybe a `setup`) in the hand, `bribe` scales `exposureRiskOf` (`bribeMul`), `ads` adds followers at once, `provoke` forces tomorrow's incident on the tensest pair (`advanceRelations`), `paparazzi` forces tomorrow's hunt find, `giveaway` makes today's issue reach every resident in `publish`; UI: the `cash-open` button, `cash-chip`s and the abilities in `abilityBar` (`hud-abilities`, inside `hudControls` between «Сводка» and «В печать»: icons with `ability-badge`s and tooltips), the `cash` modal (`cashContent`). Cards have `rarity` (common/rare/legendary), `tags` and `starter`; `modifiersOf(c, upgrades, levels)` applies `levelEffects` (benefits × `cardLevelPower[level-1]`, drawbacks kept; `LOWER_IS_BETTER` fields) and `synergies.json` sets (three cards of a tag → its effects, `Modifiers.synergies`); new effects `moneyPerDay` (in `collectIncome`), `salaryMul`/`investigationCostMul` (`upkeepItems`), `investigationTurnsAdd` (`investigationTurns(c, r, mods)`), `tensionMul` (`tensionShift`), `incidentMul` (`advanceRelations`), `crimeHeatMul` (`heatCrime`), `stainMul` (`huntStainOf`), `lureCostMul` (`lureCost`) and abilities (`UpgradeDef.ability` {cooldown, cost}, `useAbility`/`abilityBlock`/`abilitiesOf`, `state.abilityReady`: `night_shift` = `findNews` with `{only, sure}`, `sensation_day` raises the best story a tier without a second sensation); `drawback` is the red minus, `icon` the card glyph; `mole` (`seeRivalPlan`) shows the rival's plan in its plaque and `checkMole` may lose it (`moleRisk`, `molePenalty`, `TurnReport.moleCaught`). New modifiers: `scaryImpactMul` (in `impactOf` for harmful stories), `trustGainMul`, `acquisitionMul`, `frontReadMul` (channel), `wordOfMouthMul`, `debunkFindAdd`. UI: `shopContent` (style chips, the fork side by side, the deck grid with `deck-tag`s and «Обновить», owned chips) and the `abilityBar` above the paper (`renderAbilities`; a night-shift story lands as a brief).
